Farm, flock and flowers.

A small family place in the Central Valley. Geese and poultry hatched here, dahlias cut in the morning, and Shorthorn cattle out back.

Three things we raise

Flock

Sebastopol, Embden and Toulouse geese — hatching eggs and started goslings. Bresse eggs, and mixed duck and chicken eggs from the laying flock.

See the flock →

Flowers

Dahlias — cut stems and bunches through the season, and tubers when the clumps come up. A longer season here than most places get.

Farm

Registered Shorthorn and Shorthorn Plus cattle, plus a couple of club cows.

How it works

Tell us what you're after

Call (559) 836-2880 or send a note with what you want and roughly when. No cart, no checkout — just a conversation.

We'll tell you what's ready

Hatches and blooms run on their own schedule. We'll tell you what's available now and what's coming, and put you on the list if it isn't yet.

Sort out the details

Pick up at the farm, or ask about shipping eggs. We'll have everything packed and ready when you come.
