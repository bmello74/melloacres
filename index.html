Welcome to our little farm in Hanford, California

Farm, flock and flowers.

A little family farm in the heart of the Central Valley, where the geese are hatched by hand, the dahlias are cut at sunrise, and there is always something growing.

What we love to grow

Flock

Sebastopol, Embden and Toulouse geese, with hatching eggs and sweet started goslings. Bresse eggs too, and lovely mixed duck and chicken eggs.

How to bring something home

Tell us what you have in mind

Give us a call at (559) 836-2880 or send a note. Every order here begins with a friendly conversation — we love hearing what you're planning.

We'll share what's ready

Our hatches and our blooms keep their own beautiful schedule. We'll let you know what's ready today, what's coming soon, and save your spot on the list.

Come and collect

Arrange a time to visit the farm, or ask us about shipping eggs. Everything will be packed up and waiting for you.
