What to tell us

The more specific you are, the more useful our answer is. A note like “six Sebastopol eggs, sometime in spring, I'll drive out” gets a real answer the same day.

Eggs and goslings

  • Which breed — Sebastopol, Embden, Toulouse, Bresse, or mixed duck or chicken.
  • Hatching eggs or started goslings. (We don't sell day-old goslings.)
  • How many, and roughly when.
  • Whether you're picking up or asking about shipping.

Flowers and tubers

  • Cut flowers or tubers — they're available at opposite ends of the season.
  • The date you need them, if there is one.
  • Single stems, mixed bunches, or a bucket for your own arranging.

Cattle

  • Registered breeding stock, Shorthorn Plus, or a club calf.
  • What the animal is for, and when you need it.
