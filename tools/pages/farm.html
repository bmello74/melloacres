The farm

Shorthorn cattle

Primarily registered Shorthorn and Shorthorn Plus, with a couple of club cows in the mix. A small herd, handled often and known individually.

Registered Shorthorn

Purebred, registered cattle — one of the oldest improved breeds, and still one of the most docile to work around.

Shorthorn Plus

Recorded percentage Shorthorn cattle, bringing Shorthorn disposition and maternal traits into a crossbred package.

Club cows

A couple of show-type cattle in the herd — the kind of animal a kid can halter, work with daily, and take into a ring.

Why Shorthorns

Temperament, first and foremost. On a small place where the cattle share the ground with geese, chickens and a flower patch, and where someone is out among them every day, a calm animal isn't a luxury.

Shorthorns bring that, along with good mothering, easy calving and the kind of even, steady growth that doesn't need to be forced. The registered side keeps the paperwork honest for anyone buying breeding stock; the Plus cattle carry the same disposition into crossbred calves.

Looking for cattle?

The herd is small, so what's available changes with the calf crop rather than sitting on a list. Whether you're after registered breeding stock or a club calf for a kid's project, tell us what you're looking for and when you need it, and we'll tell you straight whether we have it.
