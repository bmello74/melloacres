Our flowers

Dahlias, cut fresh each morning

Flowers by the stem and by the armful all season long, and tubers once the clumps come up in the fall. Everything follows the seasons here — and our season in Hanford is a generous one.

Cut flowers

Gathered in the cool of the morning and ready the very same day. Single stems, mixed bunches, or a whole bucket if you love to arrange them yourself.

Fresh blooms

Whatever is opening in the patch that week — and it changes constantly, which is half the joy of growing dahlias.

Tubers

Divided from our own healthy clumps at the end of the season, so you can grow these same beauties in your own garden.

A wonderfully long season

Nearly everywhere, dahlias are a summer-into-fall flower. Here in the Central Valley our warm weather lingers, and the patch keeps giving long after other gardens have finished.

That means we have blooms to share over a much longer stretch of the year than most growers can manage — so it's always worth asking, even late in the season. The answer is lovelier than you might expect.

Tubers come around the other way. Once the plants have finished for the year we lift and divide the clumps, and those go out ahead of the next planting season — a little something to look forward to over the winter.
