About

A small place, run close to the ground

Mello Acres sits in Hanford, in the middle of California's Central Valley. It's a family farm, owned by Michelle Mello, and it does three things.

Farm, flock and flowers — that's not a slogan somebody wrote for us. It's a fair description of what's out the back door.

Flock

Sebastopol, Embden and Toulouse geese in their own pens, Bresse chickens, and a mixed laying flock of ducks and hens. Eggs are collected here and set here; goslings are brooded here until they're old enough to go somewhere else. Nothing is drop-shipped from a hatchery with our name on the box.

Flowers

A dahlia patch that runs longer than most, because the Valley stays warm longer than most. Cut flowers through the season, and tubers divided off our own clumps when the plants are done.

Farm

Registered Shorthorn and Shorthorn Plus cattle, and a couple of club cows. A small herd, handled often enough that they're all known individually.

How we sell

By conversation. There's no cart on this site and there isn't going to be one. Hatching eggs, goslings, flowers, tubers and cattle all run on a schedule set by the birds, the weather and the calf crop — not by an inventory system. So you ask, and we tell you honestly what we've got and when.

That also means we'll tell you when the answer is no, or not yet, or you'd be better off waiting three weeks. That's the trade for not having a checkout button.
